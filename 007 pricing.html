Questions

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes. Plus runs for the period you paid for and simply stops renewing. Nothing you created is deleted — you drop back to the Free limits and everything stays where it is.

What happens to my data if I stay on Free?

It stays yours. Free is a real plan, not a trial. You keep the shared dashboard, tasks, events and expenses forever; only the AI allowance and member count are capped.

How do I pay from Bangladesh?

bKash, Nagad or any Visa/Mastercard and local bank card, all through SSLCommerz. You never type card details into Wisal itself — payment happens on the gateway’s own secure page.

Is one subscription enough for the whole family?

Yes. Plus applies to your household, not to each person. Everyone you invite shares it.

What does “bring your own Gemini key” mean?

If you have your own Google Gemini API key you can add it in Settings, and the assistant will use that instead. Useful if you want heavier AI use or full control over where requests go.
