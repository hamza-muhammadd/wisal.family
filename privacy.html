Privacy & Terms

Effective 6 August 2026 · Last updated 6 August 2026

Your family's life belongs to your family. Wisal was built so a household can keep its plans, health notes, money and memories in one place without handing that life to anyone else. This page explains exactly what is stored, where it goes, and how you erase it.

The short version

  • Your data is stored on your device first.
  • If you sign in, a copy syncs to your own private space so your household stays together across phones.
  • No ads. No trackers. No analytics. Your data is never sold or rented.
  • Only the AI assistant sends data outside — and only when you ask it something.
  • Card and wallet details are handled by the payment gateway. Wisal never sees them.
  • You can delete everything at any time, from inside the app.

Wisal is an independent app made by Hamza, based in Bangladesh. In this policy, “Wisal”, “we” and “us” mean the maker of the app. “You” means the person using it.

If anything here is unclear, or you want your data removed, write to hamzamuhammad.trd@gmail.com. Every message gets an answer.

3. Where your data lives

On your device

By default everything stays in your phone or browser's local storage. If you never sign in, your data never leaves the device.

In your private cloud space

When you sign in, an encrypted copy syncs to Supabase, our database and authentication provider. Row-Level Security means each account can only ever read the household it owns or was invited into with a code. Nobody else — including other Wisal users — can reach your household's rows.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest by the provider.

4. The AI assistant — please read this

What it can do

The assistant is not a general chatbot bolted on the side. It reads a summary of your household so it can act on it. Today it can:

  • Answer from your own data — what is due this week, how much is left in the budget, whose appointment is next.
  • Create things for you — add tasks, events, expenses, shopping items or health entries from a plain sentence, in English or Bangla.
  • Notice what you would miss — overdue items, a medicine running out, a birthday coming up, spending running ahead of the month.
  • Summarise the week — a short picture of how the household actually went.
  • Suggest next steps — and, when it proposes an action, wait for you to approve it before anything is saved.

More is planned. As the assistant gains the ability to reach outside the app on your behalf, this page will say so plainly before that ships — not after.

What leaves the app

When you send the assistant a message, that message plus a summary of your family data — things like how many open tasks you have, upcoming events, this month's spending, your shopping list — goes to Google's Gemini API so it can answer usefully.

This happens only when you send a message to the assistant. If you never open it, nothing is ever sent.

What is never sent: your Secure Vault, the PIN-protected private space, your passwords, and your family chat messages.

You may add your own Google Gemini API key in Settings. If you do, requests go directly from your device using your key, under your own agreement with Google.

Google processes this as a service provider. See Google's Privacy Policy. Please avoid pasting information into the assistant that you would not want processed by a third party.

5. Payments and subscriptions

Wisal Free is free forever. Wisal Plus is an optional paid plan for a household.

Payments are processed by SSLCommerz, a payment gateway licensed in Bangladesh, which handles bKash, Nagad and card payments. You enter your wallet or card details on their secure page, never inside Wisal.

Wisal never sees, stores or transmits your card number, wallet PIN or OTP. What we keep is only the outcome: which plan you are on, the billing period, the date it renews, and a transaction reference so we can help if a payment goes wrong.

Refunds and payment disputes: write to us and we will look at it honestly and quickly.

6. Keeping accounts real

Two services help make sure a human, not a bot, is creating an account:

  • Cloudflare Turnstile — a privacy-first CAPTCHA on the sign-in screen. It checks the browser, not you, and Cloudflare states it does not use it to track people across sites.
  • Resend — delivers the confirmation email that proves your address is really yours. It receives your email address and the message itself, nothing more.

7. Who we share data with

We do not sell, rent or trade your data. It is shared only with the services that make the app work:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage. Holds your synced household data.
  • Google (Gemini API) — processes AI assistant requests, as described above.
  • SSLCommerz — processes subscription payments.
  • Cloudflare — bot protection on sign-in.
  • Resend — sends confirmation and account emails.
  • Vercel — serves the website and app. Receives standard server request data such as IP address, as any web host does.

We may disclose data if legally required to, but we have no interest in doing so and would tell you where the law allows.

8. Your PIN-protected spaces

The Secure Vault and the private Relationship space are locked with a PIN that only you know. We cannot see inside them, cannot recover them, and cannot reset your PIN. If you forget it, that content is gone. Please keep a backup of anything irreplaceable.

9. Deleting your data

You are always in control. From inside the app:

  • Settings → Reset — erases everything stored on that device.
  • Settings → Account → Delete account & data — erases your cloud copy too, permanently.

You can also email hamzamuhammad.trd@gmail.com and your account and all associated data will be deleted within 30 days. Backups you exported yourself are files in your own hands — we cannot reach those.

10. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • See what data we hold about you
  • Export it — the app has a one-tap backup export
  • Correct anything wrong
  • Delete everything

Just write to us. There is no form to fill and no charge.

11. Children

Wisal is designed for adults managing a household and is not directed at children. Parents and guardians may create profiles for their children within their own household, and are responsible for that information. We do not knowingly collect data directly from children. If you believe a child has created their own account, contact us and we will remove it.

12. How long we keep data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Delete your account and it is removed from our systems, with backups cycling out within 30 days. Payment records may be kept longer where financial record-keeping rules require it.

13. Security

We use HTTPS/TLS everywhere, encryption at rest, Row-Level Security so households cannot see each other, bot protection on sign-in, and PIN protection for your most private spaces. No system is perfect — if we ever discover a breach affecting your data, we will tell you promptly and plainly.

Owners and admins can invite and manage members; every member is responsible for what they add. Children's profiles are managed by their guardians. Wisal is built for adults running a home.

Wisal is provided with care, as-is — please keep backups of what matters most. Continued use means you agree to these terms.
